Pick a shape, type in the sizes you want (in millimetres), then download your STL file. Works offline.

Drag to turn it. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Double-click to reset the view.
Sizes are in millimetres (25.4 mm = 1 inch). The model is saved sitting flat on the bed, ready to open in your slicer. STL works in every slicer. 3MF is Bambu Studio's own format.
